Complaints &
Dispute Resolution.

We aim to address concerns relating to our financial services fairly and promptly.

How to make a complaint

Please send an email to hello@koivest.co.nz with the subject “Complaint – KOIVEST”. Explain the issue, the relevant service or transaction, and the outcome you are seeking. Please do not include passwords or sensitive identity documents in an ordinary email.

What happens next

We will review your complaint, contact you if further information is needed and work towards a fair resolution. We will explain our response and any further options available to you.

Independent dispute resolution

KOIVEST LIMITED is a member of the Financial Dispute Resolution Service (FDRS), an independent external dispute resolution scheme in New Zealand. If a complaint about an eligible financial service remains unresolved, you may be able to refer it to FDRS. Its service is free for eligible consumers.

FDRS website: fdrs.org.nz

Scheme eligibility, complaint processes and referral criteria are determined by FDRS. This information does not restrict any other legal rights you may have.
